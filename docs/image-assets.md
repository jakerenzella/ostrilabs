# EmuLabs SuperHealth artwork

The site uses React SVG illustrations in `src/components/illustrations/`. The shared palette is forest green, cream, blue, orange and yellow, with rounded shapes and dark outlines.

## Product — `TabletBottle.tsx`

A forest-green supplement bottle with a ribbed blue cap and a cream label reading “EMULABS”, “SuperHealth” and “DAILY TABLETS”. Two round, scored tablets sit beside the bottle. The same illustration appears in the hero, product section, pricing card and SuperHealth Check result.

The product is always shown and described as tablets. Use **EmuLabs** in regular text; **EMULABS** is also acceptable on packaging.

## Characters and scenery

- `Ellie.tsx`: the founder, used in the hero and closing section.
- `Portrait.tsx`: emu portraits with accessories for team members and study participants.
- `Scene.tsx`: rolling hills, gum trees and the creek.
- `Sticker.tsx`, `Wave.tsx` and `Icons.tsx`: supporting decoration and icons.
- `Chart.tsx`: the fictional study’s vitality chart.
