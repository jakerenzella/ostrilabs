# Emulabs artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, one request per image. No variants or retries. The original PNG outputs were converted to WebP at quality 85 with Sharp for delivery. All three were visually inspected. These are fictional scenes, not documentary photographs.

Shared direction: photoreal editorial artwork with subtle anthropomorphic animals, premium wellness styling, olive green, cream, and warm Australian golden light. Recognisable shaggy brown emus with small blue-black heads and feathered necks; no ostriches, bare pink necks or humans.
